Slidewinder

A puzzle game that mixes old favorites: piece shapes from Tetris, the slide and merge of 2048, color groups from SameGame, and fixed targets from Dr. Mario. Remove all cores before the time runs out.

How to play

  • Drag one of your two pieces to a side of the board. It enters from that side, and all tiles slide away from you. Outlines show where the cells land.
  • Tap a piece to rotate it. The piece you play is replaced by the next piece.
  • Two tiles of the same color and value that meet merge, and the value doubles.
  • A same-color group whose values add up to 5 or more is removed. The tiles then slide again, which can start a chain.
  • Cores are bolted to the board and never move. A match removes them.
  • After each move, gray stones appear on the side the piece came from. A match removes the stones next to it.
  • A chain of 2 or more gives a tap. A tap removes any group of 2 or more.
  • Time left at the end of a level gives bonus points. The game ends when time runs out or no piece fits.

Keys: 1/2 choose a piece, Z/X rotate, arrows choose the slide direction, Q/E move along the side, Space play, T tap mode.

How it was built

Slidewinder was designed and written in a conversation with Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, in Claude Code. It is TypeScript built with Vite. All graphics are SVG drawn from code, with no game engine and no image files. A test bot that tries every possible move set the difficulty of the levels. The source is on GitHub.
