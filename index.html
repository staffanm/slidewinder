How to play

Remove all tiles with a ring (cores) to clear the level.

  1. Place the piece anywhere it fits.
  2. Slide all tiles in one direction. Cores do not move.
  • Two tiles of the same color and value that meet merge, and the value doubles.
  • A same-color group whose values add up to 5 or more is removed. The tiles then slide again, which can start a chain.
  • After each slide, gray stones appear on the side the tiles moved away from. More stones come at higher levels.
  • Stones do not match or merge. A match removes the stones next to it.
  • A chain of 2 or more gives a tap. A tap removes any group of 2 or more.
  • The game ends when no piece fits.

Mouse: click to place, wheel to rotate, right-click to tap, drag or click an arrow to slide.
Keys: arrows move the piece or slide, Space place, Z/X rotate, C hold, T tap mode.
Touch: tap to place, swipe to slide.
